## Examined and Decided NOT a Defect

1. **Storage of dates and shift times as strings (`YYYY-MM-DD`, `HH:MM`)**:
   - `DATA_MODEL.md` explicitly specifies: *"Calendar dates and times of day are plain strings on purpose: a date is not an instant, and turning it into one forces a time zone on it."* Keeping `date`, `joined_on`, `shift_start`, and `shift_end` as formatted strings avoids unnecessary timezone conversions and preserves wall-clock intent.

2. **Presence of `history: []` initialized to an empty array on punch-in**:
   - Initializing `history` to `[]` matches the MongoDB document specification in `DATA_MODEL.md` (Sample 1–7) and OpenAPI contract (`HistoryEntry[]`), indicating an audit trail that has not yet had manual corrections.

3. **Collection names (`employees`, `attendance_logs`)**:
   - Both collection names directly reflect the naming required by `DATA_MODEL.md` and `sample_seed.py`.
